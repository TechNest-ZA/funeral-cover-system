Your digital membership book

Instead of a paper book that can be lost, torn or left behind, your payments and cover live on a private link you can open from your phone, any time.

When you join, you get a link and a QR code that open your own book — your plan, what it includes, every payment you've made, and who's covered under your membership. Save it, screenshot it, or scan the code again whenever you need it.

You manage your own family from the same book — add a dependent or remove one yourself, no staff approval needed. And if you ever lose the link, our staff can issue you a fresh one on the spot.

Questions about your book

What if I lose my phone or the link?
Come in or call us. Staff can issue you a fresh link, and the old one stops working from that moment.
Can anyone else see my book?
Only someone with your specific link can open it, so treat it like you would a bank card — don't share it publicly.
Do I need a password or account to log in?
No. There's nothing to remember — the link itself is what opens your book.
Is this instead of a paper record, or as well as it?
