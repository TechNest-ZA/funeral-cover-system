Choosing between them

Is family included on every plan, or only Extended?
Single covers one person. Family and Extended add your household and, on Extended, your parents and in-laws too — the tiers change who's covered as well as the level of service.
What's actually different about Extended?
Up to 10 dependants including parents anywhere in South Africa, transport home to the village, and a contribution towards a tombstone for the unveiling — the benefits unique to Extended.
Which plan do most people choose?
Family is our most popular plan — it covers a typical household of six and adds the tent and catering without paying for the top tier.
Can I move up to a higher plan later?
Talk to us about it directly — call or come in and we'll work out what makes sense for your situation.
