Home/Cover for your family

Does this cover my whole family?

Yes — every plan covers your immediate family under one membership, and you can add dependents yourself at any time through your digital membership book.

There's no separate sign-up for a spouse or child, and no extra premium for being a bigger household. When you join, you're the member — and your immediate family is covered alongside you from day one.

If your family grows, or you simply didn't add everyone when you first joined, you don't need to phone us or fill in a form. You open your own membership book on your phone and add them yourself, whenever it suits you.

Questions about family cover

How many family members can I add?
As many as make up your immediate family. There's no complicated form — you simply enter their name and how they relate to you.
Do I need to add everyone when I join, or can I do it later?
Later is fine. Plenty of members join first and add dependents in the weeks after, once they're settled in.
Does adding a family member cost extra?
No. It's included in the one membership you're already paying for.
What if a family member already has cover somewhere else?
That's fine — talk to us about your situation and we'll help you work out what makes sense for your household.
