The funeral service itself

From the moment you phone us, we arrange the practical side of the funeral — the casket, the hearse, and, depending on your plan, the tent and catering for the gathering afterwards.

Body collection, washing and mortuary storage are included from our first tier of cover, along with paperwork assistance for the death certificate. As your plan level goes up, so does the casket, the number of family cars in the hearse procession, and — from the Family plan upward — the tent, chairs and catering for the gathering at the home.

What we ask of the family is small: choose the casket, and tell us about the day. Everything else — collection, paperwork, transport, setting up — is arranged by us.

On the day itself

What's actually different between the plans?
Mainly the casket, the number of family cars, and whether the tent, chairs and catering for the gathering are included. Every plan covers body collection, paperwork and the core service.
Can we add something not included in our plan?
Talk to us and we'll tell you honestly what it would involve. We won't add anything without agreeing it with you first.
Do you handle the death certificate too?
Yes — paperwork assistance is included on every plan, not just the higher tiers.
Who do we actually speak to on the day?
A member of our own staff, present with the family from the morning of the service.
