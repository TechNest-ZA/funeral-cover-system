Funeral cover in Nelspruit, from R99 a month

Yes — we cover families across Nelspruit and the surrounding areas, and you can join tonight from your phone without coming to the branch.

One membership gives you both parts of the cover: cash in your family's hands within the same week, and the funeral itself — the casket, the hearse, and, depending on your plan, the tent and catering for the gathering afterwards — arranged and paid for by the parlor that will carry it out.

Questions people ask before joining

How do I actually join?
From your phone — choose a plan, enter your details, and pay. Your membership is active as soon as your first payment clears, and you'll have a digital membership card straight away.

Yes. Your immediate family is included under your one membership, and you can add dependents yourself at any time — no extra sign-up, no separate premium.
Do I need to visit the branch to sign up?
No. Everything can be done from your phone. You're always welcome to walk in too — no appointment needed.
