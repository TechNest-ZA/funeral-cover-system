Tombstones & memorials

Extended includes a contribution towards the tombstone for the unveiling, so it isn't a separate cost you have to find all at once a year later.

The unveiling usually comes around a year after the burial, and families are often caught out by it — the funeral itself was arranged and covered, but a tombstone is a real, separate cost that tends to be remembered only a month or two before the date.

Extended builds a contribution towards that cost into your membership, alongside the fuller funeral service and the cash payout the plan already includes. Call us for the specifics of how the contribution is applied to your situation.
