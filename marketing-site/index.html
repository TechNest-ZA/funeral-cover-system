Why families cover themselves

A funeral does not wait for you to find the money.

Everyone knows what a funeral costs. Fewer people talk about what happens in the five days when the money is not there — because the money is needed immediately, and the family is in no state to negotiate for it.

We are not writing this to frighten anybody. We are writing it because we sit with families in that exact week, and the ones who joined a cover years ago are having a different week from the ones who did not.

What actually happens when someone dies

You phone one number. From that moment we handle everything, and we tell you exactly where things stand at every step.

The first call

You phone one number, day or night

A person answers — not a machine. We take a few details and come to you, whether that is a home, a hospital or another town.

Day one

We collect and care for your person

Body collection and mortuary storage are included from your first tier of cover. You are welcome to visit before the funeral.

Days two to three

We help with the death certificate

Paperwork assistance is part of every plan. We sit with you once to choose the casket and settle the order of service.

Same week

The money reaches the family

The cash part of your cover is paid within the same week, usually inside 48 hours of the certificate — before the funeral, not after it.

The day

We run the service

Casket, hearse, and — depending on your plan — the tent, chairs and catering for the gathering afterwards. One of our people is there with the family.

Common questions

Everything you need to know

Do I need a medical exam to join?
No. There's no medical exam, no doctor's letter and no questions about your health — just your ID number and your dependants' details.
Is my whole family covered under one membership?
Yes. Your immediate family is covered automatically on every plan, and you can add or remove dependants yourself at any time through your digital membership book — no forms, no calling us.
Why does natural-causes cover have a waiting period?
Accidental death is covered from your very first day as a member. Natural-causes cover begins after a six-month waiting period — standard for this kind of cover, and there to keep premiums low for everyone.
Can I switch from another funeral parlor?
Yes — moving your family across is simpler than people expect. Read how switching works →
How does the cash payout reach my family?
Once we have the death certificate and the member's ID, the cash portion is paid to your nominated beneficiary — usually within 48 hours, and before the funeral, not after it. Read how payouts work →
