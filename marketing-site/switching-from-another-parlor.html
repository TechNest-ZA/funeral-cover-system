Switching from another parlor or burial society

If you're not happy with your current cover, moving your family across to us is simpler than people expect — and there's no reason to keep paying for something you don't trust.

A lot of people stay with a society they're unsure about simply because switching feels complicated, or because their record book is the only proof they have of years of payments. We understand that hesitation — which is exactly why we built a proper digital record instead of another paper book that can be lost or damaged.

Switching means joining as a new member here — you choose a plan, add your family, and get real, checkable cover from your first payment. It usually takes less time than the call you'd make to query your current society.

What switching actually involves

Do I lose anything by switching?
We won't pretend otherwise — funeral cover isn't savings, so years paid elsewhere don't transfer as a balance. What we can do is make sure you're not still paying, month after month, into cover you no longer trust.
Can I move my whole family at once?
Yes. Add everyone while you're already sitting down to join — it's no extra work to do it in one go.
How do I actually start?
Join from your phone the same way any new member would, or come into the branch and we'll do it with you.
Will my old society find out?
We won't contact them — that's between you and them. It's worth formally ending your old membership yourself, though, so there's no confusion about arrears later.
